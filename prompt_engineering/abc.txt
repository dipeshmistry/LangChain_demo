Use Case: AI-Based Candidate Matcher with Automated Outreach Emails
Goal:
Identify relevant candidates for upskilling programs and automatically generate personalized outreach emails based on:

Candidate matching (based on past training history and current skills).

Current position and tech stack (extracted from LinkedIn).

Program relevance (matching upskilling courses to their skill gaps).

Added Benefit:
Streamlined engagement with potential candidates, automated messaging, and improved lead nurturing for upskilling.

Key Workflow with Outreach Email Generation
Match Candidates: Use vector embeddings to match candidates based on their:

Current position (from LinkedIn).

Current tech stack (from LinkedIn).

Training history (from your portal).

Skill gaps (based on the tech stack mismatch between their role and your upskilling program).

Email Personalization: Once you’ve identified the relevant candidates, use a Natural Language Generation (NLG) model (like GPT) to draft personalized emails.

Email Template Design
Use a template with placeholders to dynamically insert personalized content
